  WHY MEDIAN: Median is robust to single-stock outliers. A sector with 9 flat stocks and 1 stock at +30% gets a median score ≈0%, correctly reflecting that the sector as a whole did not move. A mean would give a misleading +3% sector score. This is explicit PRD design intent.
  Top constituent = stock with highest absolute pct_change (for both price and volume rankings).
  Sectors below SECTOR_MIN_CONSTITUENTS=3 valid data points are excluded.
  Results sorted descending by sector_score.

  VOLUME RANKING ALGORITHM:
  Same sector/company structure. Looks back lookback_days=20 prior trading sessions (calendar buffer: 60+ days of price_data fetched to guarantee 20 trading days).
  today_sector_vol = sum of constituent volumes for target_date (NULL/0 volumes excluded).
  prior_20d_sector_daily_totals = sector daily totals for up to 20 most-recent sessions strictly before target_date, sorted descending by date, then truncated to lookback_days.
  volume_20d_avg = mean(prior_20d_sector_daily_totals).
  volume_ratio = round(today_sector_vol / volume_20d_avg × 100, 2).
  Sector excluded if: today_sector_vol=0, no prior days, volume_20d_avg=0, or <SECTOR_MIN_CONSTITUENTS price-change entries.
  Top constituent = highest absolute intraday pct_change (same definition as price ranking).
  UNITS: volume_ratio is dimensionless (%) — today expressed as % of trailing 20-day norm. 150 means today's volume is 1.5× the 20-day average.

  EDGE CASES HANDLED: open=0/NULL skips pct_change; volume=NULL/0 skips daily contribution; fewer prior days than lookback_days uses what is available (partial history is valid — it is the earliest days of live operation); all-zero prior volume excludes sector; today=0 volume excludes sector.
